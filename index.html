I am an undergraduate student at Yonsei University, double majoring in Physics and Electrical & Electronic Engineering. Recently, I joined CVLAB in the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI at KAIST as an undergradute researcher, advised by Prof. Seungryong Kim.

My early experiences on discrete diffusion led me to view diffusion language models as combining two components: diffusion as a generative framework and language modeling as a autoregressive (AR) framework. This experience led my research interests beyond the language domain toward areas where two central axes of generative modeling—diffusion and autoregressive—meet. From this perspective, I am particularly interested in AR video diffusion and discrete diffusion.
